  - question: "What's the difference between stump grinding and stump removal?"
    answer: "Stump grinding cuts the stump down below the soil line and turns it into wood chips, while most of the deep roots stay in the ground and break down over time. Full stump removal pulls out the stump and root ball, which leaves a large hole and disturbs more of the yard. For most Frederick yards, grinding is the practical way to get rid of a stump. We'll talk through options at the estimate."
  - question: "How deep do you grind stumps in Frederick, MD?"
    answer: "We typically grind 6-8 inches below the surface, depending on stump size. That gets the stump below the soil line so you can replant, resod, or build over the spot. Tell us your plans at the free on-site estimate."
  - question: "Can I plant grass or a new tree where a stump was ground?"
    answer: "Yes. Grass and sod do well once the chips are cleared and the hole is filled with topsoil, because chips left in the soil can pull nitrogen as they break down.
