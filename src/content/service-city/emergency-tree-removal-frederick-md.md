stormEmergency:
  heading: "Power lines, and what to do while you wait"
  body: |
    If a tree is on a power line, or a line is on the ground, call your electric utility before you call anyone else to cut the tree. In Frederick and Frederick County that is the company on your electric bill. Also call 911 if a line is sparking, blocking a road, or you smell gas. Stay well back. Do not touch the tree, the line, a fence, or a vehicle that the line is touching. Do not spray it with a hose.

    Snyder's does not cut trees off live lines. Once the utility says the line is safe, call [(301) 288-1677](tel:+13012881677) if you have not already, and we will take the tree from there.

    While you wait for a crew, keep people and pets inside or well away from the tree. If the house was hit, use a room that is not under the damaged part, or leave if the structure feels unsafe. Do not climb, do not pull on limbs, and do not run a generator indoors. If you can do it from a safe distance, take photos for your records before anything is moved. Your insurer decides the claim. The photos are just so you have a clear record of where the tree landed and what it hit.
